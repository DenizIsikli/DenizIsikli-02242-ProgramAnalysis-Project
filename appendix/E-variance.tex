\section{Variance of Random Testing and Timings}
\label{app:variance}

We ran random testing with 20 seeds on the 19 pair and domain runs that really differ
(\code{evaluation/seed\_stats.py}, on a laptop with an Intel Core i5-1038NG7). On the small
domain every seed found the difference (180 of 180), since 1000 inputs cover at most 49. On
the large domain the detection rate was 53\% (105 of 200 seeded runs) and depended on how many
inputs differ: 20 of 20 for \code{absVal}, \code{justAdd/justMulitply} and
\code{ladder/\_changed}, 12 of 20 for \code{belowTwo}, 9 of 20 for \code{loopDiv/\_guarded}, 6 of
20 for \code{sign}, \code{reciprocal} and both \code{checkBeforeDivideByN} variants, and 0 of 20
for \code{ratio}, which differs on one input in 4,004,001. A \chg{} answer from testing is
therefore luck on exactly the single-point differences that motivate this work.

Five repeats of every timing give a median total of about 9\,s for the hybrid and 2.4\,s for
one SMT query on that laptop. They come from the same laptop as
\cref{tab:results}.
