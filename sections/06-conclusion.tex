\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether an edit preserves the behaviour of a method, and where it does not. Our
checker decides boxes of inputs with interval analyses and sound verdict rules, and asks a
bit-vector SMT solver only about return values. On 20 pairs and two domains it never gave a
wrong verdict and certified 96\% of the inputs on average, against 45\% for one SMT query
and 2\% for differential testing, including loop pairs that symbolic execution rejects.
A relational domain is the next step.
