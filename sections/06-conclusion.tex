\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether an edit preserves the behaviour of a method, and where it does not. Our
checker decides boxes of inputs with interval analyses and sound verdict rules. It asks a
bit-vector SMT solver only about return values. On 20 pairs and two domains, no verdict
contradicted the ground truth. The mean certified coverage was 96\%, against 45\% for one SMT
query and 2\% for differential testing. The checker also decides loop pairs that our symbolic
executor rejects.
